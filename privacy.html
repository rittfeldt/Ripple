Ripple – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Ripple is a calculator app. It is built to work entirely on your device. Ripple does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your calculation history, settings (such as theme, number of decimals, sounds and haptics) and whether you have unlocked Ripple Pro are stored locally on your iPhone. If you use the Home Screen widget, a small amount of this information (your theme, the widget's current calculation and your Pro status) is shared between the app and the widget on the same device. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Ripple Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Payment details and purchase records are processed by Apple, and the developer does not receive your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.

Third parties

Ripple does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Children

Because Ripple does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children either.

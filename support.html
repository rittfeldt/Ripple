How do I edit a number in my history?

Tap a number in the history, type the new value with the keypad, and press = when you are done. Every row below that builds on it updates automatically. AC undoes the change. Tap an operator to switch between +, −, × and ÷.

How do I restore my Ripple Pro purchase?

Open Settings (the slider icon) and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it. If you share purchases through Family Sharing, Pro unlocks automatically for family members.

The Home Screen widget says it is locked

The mini calculator widget is part of Ripple Pro. Open Ripple and check that Pro is unlocked in Settings. Then touch and hold the widget, or remove it and add it again.

How do I clear my history?

Tap the trash can to clear the history and the current calculation. In Settings you can also choose to clear history every day or each time the app is launched.

How do I add Ripple to Control Center?

Swipe down from the top-right corner, tap + in the top-left corner, choose Add a Control and pick Ripple.
